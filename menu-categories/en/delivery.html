Delivery and payment

Delivery

Every piece is placed in branded packaging and a cardboard shipping box ♡ We dispatch orders every weekday when they are placed before 16:00.

  • Nova Poshta delivery across Ukraine to a branch or parcel locker, within 3 days.
  • Nova Poshta courier delivery to your address.
  • Collection from our showroom: 63/28 Saksahanskoho Street, Kyiv.

The buyer pays delivery charges at the carrier’s rates. We cover delivery for fully paid orders of UAH 10,000 or more ♡

Payment

No payment is taken at checkout. After you place an order, a manager will contact you to confirm availability and the details.

  • Order with payment. A manager will confirm your order and send a payment link for Apple Pay or a bank card.
  • Order with a consultation. You do not need to pay immediately. A manager will contact you during the next working hours, answer your questions and arrange a convenient payment method: full payment or cash on delivery.

Cash on delivery with a UAH 200 prepayment is arranged with the manager during a consultation. You can examine and try on the jewellery at a Nova Poshta branch. The prepayment is included in the order total; you pay the balance when collecting the parcel. The buyer pays Nova Poshta’s cash-on-delivery fee of UAH 20 plus 2% of the transferred amount.

Showroom collection can be paid for in cash or by card on site.

How to place an order

We know how important it is to see jewellery in person, so you can choose the option that suits you:

  1. Add a piece to your bag and proceed to checkout. Enter your name, telephone number and delivery method.
  2. Choose “Order with payment” if you have made your choice, or “Order with a consultation” if you would first like to see a video, check a size or discuss the details.
  3. A manager will contact you during the next working hours, confirm availability and send a payment link or arrange cash on delivery.

You can also message us on Instagram or Telegram. Our consultants can help you choose a piece and place an order.

Packaging

Gift certificates

A perfect gift is the freedom to choose. If you are unsure about the size or design, a LIMÉRENCE gift certificate offers that choice ♡

You can choose any piece from our range. If it costs more than the certificate value, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is not refunded.

Warranty and care

All LIMÉRENCE jewellery is made in Ukraine. Each piece bears the hallmark of the Assay Chamber of Ukraine and matches the fineness stated in its description: 585 or 750 gold, or 925 silver. The mark is applied mechanically or by laser.

The warranty period is 6 months with the jewellery passport and receipt. It covers manufacturing defects, including stone setting, clasp repairs, jewellery component repairs, broken chains and pendant component repairs when the components are present. Cleaning and polishing during a warranty repair are also included. The customer pays transport costs.

Repairs are chargeable when damage results from incorrect use or careless handling, including impacts, deformation, mechanical chain damage, and lost jewellery components or stones.

Under Resolution No. 172 of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine, jewellery made from precious metals, precious stones, precious stones of organic origin and semi-precious stones, when of satisfactory quality, is not eligible for exchange or return.

When we replace jewellery or issue a refund is explained on the Returns and exchanges page.

How to care for your jewellery

  • Remove jewellery before exercising, swimming, visiting a sauna or beach, using household chemicals or cosmetics, and before sleeping.
  • Avoid contact with iodine, chlorine, sulphur, alcohol-based substances, dyes and ammonia.
  • Store pieces in separate soft pouches or padded jewellery boxes. Do not keep several rings together or use metal holders.
  • Do not scrub jewellery with stiff brushes or leave pieces with stones in the sun, as ultraviolet light can dull stones.
  • Once a month, wash jewellery for a few minutes in warm soapy water, then wipe it with a soft towel or microfibre cloth. Do not use a needle to remove dust beneath a stone, as this may loosen it.
  • Take rings to a jeweller for professional cleaning once a year. Our showroom offers free ultrasonic jewellery cleaning for our customers.
